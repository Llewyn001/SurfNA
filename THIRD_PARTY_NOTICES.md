# Third-party provenance

- SurfDock: https://github.com/Intelligent-Drug-Discovery-Lab/SurfDock — upstream surface-based diffusion code. Cao, D., Chen, M., Zhang, R. et al., *Nature Methods* (2024), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02516-y. The upstream project identifies its license as MIT; its original copyright notice is retained in `licenses/SurfDock.txt`.
- DiffDock: https://github.com/gcorso/DiffDock — diffusion docking components and associated geometric utilities. Its MIT license is preserved in `licenses/DiffDock.txt` in this package.
- Existing Apache notices in individual source files remain applicable. Dependencies and molecular preparation executables are not relicensed by this package.

Upstream notices do not resolve the SurfNA authors' own code/model/data licensing choice. See `LICENSE_PENDING.md`.
